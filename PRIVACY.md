# Impulse Vault — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 September 2026_

Impulse Vault is a browser extension that adds a cooling-off period to online shopping. This policy explains what it handles and what it doesn't.

**The short version:** everything stays in your browser. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.

## What the extension handles

To work, Impulse Vault handles the following, **on your device only**:

- **Product details from pages you vault** — the product's title, price, image address and page address, read from the store's page.
- **What you write** — the note about why you want an item, and the reason you give if you unlock one early.
- **Your vault and savings** — each item's timer and outcome, the running total of money you chose not to spend, and your settings.
- **Pages you visit on stores you've vaulted from** — the extension compares a tab's address with the products in your vault so it can apply the lock. These addresses are not recorded.
- **Clicks on buy buttons** — watched while an item is cooling off so the extension can pause the purchase. Clicks are not recorded.

## Where it's stored

In your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage`), on your device. It is not synced to your Google account and not sent to the developer or anyone else.

## Network requests

Impulse Vault does not send your data anywhere. The only requests it makes are to the stores themselves: when a cooling-off period ends, it loads that product's page from the store once to check whether the price changed. That's an ordinary request to the store's website, like opening the page yourself, and the extension adds nothing about you to it.

## What it doesn't do

- No accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry
- No advertising, and no affiliate links
- No selling, sharing, or transferring of data to third parties
- No use of data for anything other than the cooling-off feature described above

## Permissions

Impulse Vault works on a set of built-in stores. It only gets access to other websites if you choose to grant it — for a single store, or through an off-by-default setting for every shopping site. You can remove that access at any time from Chrome's extension settings.

## Your control

- **Export** all your data as a JSON file from Options → Your data.
- **Delete** everything from Options → Delete everything. This removes all stored data and all rules immediately.
- **Uninstalling** the extension removes its storage from your browser.
